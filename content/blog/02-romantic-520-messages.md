## FAQ

### What should I write in a 520 message?

Say “520” (I love you), add one specific memory or detail unique to your relationship, and end with a wish or promise. Personal specifics matter more than poetic language.

### When should I send a 520 message?

Send it on May 20 (5/20). Popular moments are 5:20 am, 5:20 pm, or 13:14 for the “forever” (1314) pairing. Any time on the day works.

### What does replying 521 mean?

521 sounds like “I'm willing” in Mandarin and is the traditional acceptance of a 520 confession. May 21 is also considered the reply day.

### Can I send 520 messages if I'm not Chinese?

Absolutely. 520 is widely understood across Chinese-speaking communities and increasingly worldwide. Explaining the meaning — 5-2-0 sounds like “I love you” in Mandarin — makes the gesture even more charming.

### What gift goes well with a 520 message?

A ¥5.20 or ¥52.00 red envelope is classic. Personalized digital gifts — a custom love song, an AI couple portrait, or a written love letter — are increasingly popular because they keep forever.
