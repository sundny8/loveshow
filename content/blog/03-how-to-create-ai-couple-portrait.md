# How to Create an AI Couple Portrait (Step-by-Step)

Creating an AI couple portrait takes about five minutes: **upload one clear, front-facing photo of each partner, pick an art style (wedding, anime, vintage, fantasy…), let the AI generate several options, then download your favorite in HD.** No studio, no photographer, no matching outfits required — the AI handles lighting, composition and styling.

This guide walks through each step, including how to pick photos that produce the best likeness and which styles work for which occasions.

## What You'll Need

- 1–2 clear photos of each partner (or one good photo of you together)
- 5 minutes
- An AI portrait tool — we'll use the [AI couple portrait generator](/en/ai-couple-portrait) here, and the same principles apply anywhere

## Step 1: Choose the Right Source Photos

Photo quality decides 80% of your result. The AI needs to clearly read each face to preserve likeness.

**Good source photos:**

- Front-facing, both eyes visible
- Even lighting on the face (daylight near a window is ideal)
- Sharp focus — no motion blur
- Face occupies a decent share of the frame

**Avoid:**

- Sunglasses, heavy shadows, or hair covering the face
- Extreme angles (looking far up/down or in profile)
- Group photos where the AI must guess who's who
- Heavy beauty filters — they erase the facial detail the AI needs

## Step 2: Pick a Style That Fits the Occasion

Can't decide? Generate one batch in two different styles — comparing real results is faster than deliberating.

## Step 3: Generate and Review

Upload your photos, select the style, and start generation. Most tools (including ours) produce a batch of variations in 30–60 seconds. When reviewing, check:

1. **Likeness** — do both faces genuinely look like you two?
2. **Hands and details** — AI has historically struggled with fingers; modern models are much better, but glance anyway.
3. **Consistency** — matching light direction and skin tones between both people.

## Step 4: Refine (Optional but Worth It)

If a result is close but not perfect, regenerate with the same settings — each pass produces new variations. Two practical tips:

- If one partner's likeness is off, swap in a different source photo of that person; results usually improve immediately.
- If the overall image looks great but a detail bugs you, an AI editor pass can fix small artifacts without regenerating everything.

## FAQ

### What photos work best for an AI couple portrait?

Front-facing, well-lit, sharp photos where both eyes are visible and nothing covers the face. Avoid sunglasses, extreme angles, heavy filters, and group shots.

### Can we make an AI couple portrait if we've never taken a photo together?

Yes. Upload separate photos of each partner and the AI composes you into one scene — a favorite feature for long-distance couples.

### How long does it take to generate an AI couple portrait?

Typically 30–60 seconds per batch after uploading. Including photo selection and style choice, the whole process takes about five minutes.

### Is an AI couple portrait good enough to print?

Yes — download the HD version and it prints cleanly at common sizes like 8×10" or A4. For large canvas prints, use the highest resolution available.

### Do AI portraits actually look like us?

With clear source photos, modern models preserve likeness well. If one face looks off, swapping in a different source photo of that person usually fixes it.
